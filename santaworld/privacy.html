Santa World Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · App: Santa World: Christmas Fun (Android com.agentcodeapps.santaworld)
Developer: AgentCodeApps · Contact: developer.agent47@gmail.com

In short: Santa World is made for children aged 2 to 6. It has no ads, no accounts, no chat, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no servers. Everything your child makes stays on the device. We don't collect, sell or share any personal information.

What stays on the device

DataWhy
Child profiles: a nickname, an avatar picture and an age groupSo Santa can greet your child and pick suitable activities
Stars, stickers, progress, wish list, helper chart and Advent doorsTo remember what your child has done
Colouring pages, drawings and toysSo your child can come back to them
Santa video messages and Santa photosMade on the device when a grown-up asks for them

This data lives in the app's private storage and is excluded from device backups. Deleting the app, or using "Delete all profiles and data" in the grown-up area, removes it.

Camera and microphone

Photo with Santa uses the camera to place Santa in your room. Talking Santa uses the microphone so Santa can repeat what your child says in a jolly voice. Both are optional and only switch on after a grown-up passes the parental gate and Android asks for permission. Camera images and voice are processed on the device as they happen. They are never sent anywhere and are not stored, except a photo that a grown-up chooses to save.

On supported phones, Photo with Santa uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Santa World Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see payment details. The app stores only whether Premium is active. Purchases can only be started from the grown-up area, behind the parental gate.

Sharing

We don't share data with anyone. A grown-up can save a picture or a Santa video to the phone's gallery, or share it using Android's share sheet. That only happens from behind the parental gate, and only when the grown-up chooses to.

Children's privacy

Santa World follows Google Play's Families policy and is designed to comply with COPPA and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Because nothing leaves the device, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question, please email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions: developer.agent47@gmail.com
